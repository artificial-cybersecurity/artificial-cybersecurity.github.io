Most of the interesting work I do never gets written down. A weekend spent
pulling apart a model's guardrails, a detection rule that finally stopped
paging at 3am, a tool that started as a 40-line script — it all lands in a
private notebook and stays there.

This site is the fix. It's a place to publish the work properly: what the
problem was, what I tried, what broke, and what I'd do differently.

<!--more-->

## What to expect

Three kinds of post, roughly:

1. **Research notes** — things I pulled apart, with enough detail to reproduce.
2. **Tooling** — code I wrote, why it exists, and how to run it.
3. **Field notes** — shorter write-ups of problems I hit in real systems.

No fixed schedule. I'd rather publish something worth reading occasionally
than something thin every week.
